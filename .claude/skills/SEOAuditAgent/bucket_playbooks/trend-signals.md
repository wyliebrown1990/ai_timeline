# Trend Signals Playbook

## Default lane

`propose`

## Action

- identify what changed in the last 14 days
- decide whether the right move is:
  - a fresh blog brief
  - an entity refresh brief
  - a human-only note because the signal is too noisy
- if it becomes a blog candidate, route the brief to `/AIBlogDraft`

## Hard refusals

- do not propose two trend posts for the same query within 30 days
- do not turn a weak spike into a grand thesis
- do not force a news peg if the signal is mostly evergreen
