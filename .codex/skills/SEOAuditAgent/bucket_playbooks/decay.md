# Decay Playbook

## Default lane

Usually `propose`; sometimes `human_only`.

## Action

- generate a refresh brief that explains what likely went stale
- call out sections that may need expanding or reframing
- recommend manual investigation if the loss looks structural rather than editorial

## Hard refusals

- never auto-edit a published blog body
- never auto-edit glossary or milestone body copy
- never recommend a redirect or kill-page action without human review
