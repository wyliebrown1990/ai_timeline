# Designer Brief: Hand-Drawn Illustration of the Amicai Evening Prompts Page (iOS Mobile)

## Project context
Amicai is a personal AI relationship-and-life journal. The **Evening Prompts** feature delivers a small set of personalized journaling prompts each night, generated from the user's recent conversations, behavioral patterns, and stated goals. Each prompt comes with a transparent "Based on:" footer that shows what the AI noticed in order to write it — so the user can see *why* the prompt is being asked.

I need a hand-drawn illustration of this feature **rendered inside an iPhone**, in mobile portrait view, with **fictional placeholder content** (the real text is private). This is the third in a marketing-illustration set — it should read as visually consistent with the existing Journal page and Daily Reflection page illustrations.

## Visual style
- Hand-drawn / Excalidraw aesthetic: pencil or fine-marker linework, slightly imperfect strokes, gentle pen-pressure variation
- Muted color tags behind the prompt category chips (soft blue, lavender, mint)
- Handwritten labels for section eyebrows; body text in a clean sketched sans
- Asymmetric, slightly wobbling rectangles for cards. Avoid pixel-perfect alignment
- Background of the app content area: off-white paper texture
- **Match the visual language of the companion `/journal` and `/daily-reflection` illustrations** so all three read as a set

## Canvas: iPhone in portrait orientation

Render a single iPhone (iPhone 15 / 16 Pro form factor) centered on a warm-cream background with subtle drop shadow under the device. Show:

- **Device chrome**: rounded-rectangle phone outline, Dynamic Island at top-center, single home indicator pill at the very bottom
- **Status bar** (top of the screen, inside the phone): `9:41` left, signal bars + Wi-Fi + 87% battery icon right
- **Above the app's content area**: standard iOS top safe area
- **Below the app's content area**: a hand-drawn Amicai bottom tab bar with five tabs (left → right): `🏠 Dashboard`, **`📓 Journal`** (active — boxed/highlighted), `💬 Chat`, `👥 Contacts`, `··· More`. Each tab has a small icon above a label.
- **Home indicator**: thin pill at the very bottom edge

Render the device approximately 720×1480 inside a canvas of approximately 1100×1900 (so the phone has comfortable margin on a sketched paper background).

## Tone & feel notes for the designer

- **This screen should feel like the end of the day.** Soft, contemplative, the kind of thing you read in bed before journaling. Not punchy or productivity-app urgent.
- The three category chips (`Today` blue, `Pattern` lavender, `Growth` mint) are the visual rhythm of the page — keep them clearly distinct so the user can scan and pick which one to write about first.
- The **"Based on:" footers are critical** — they're what makes Amicai's prompts feel earned rather than randomly generated. Render them with enough visible weight that a viewer of the illustration immediately understands "the AI shows its work." Italic + slightly smaller + muted gray is the right register.
- The streak / encouragement banner at the top should feel **gentle**, not gamified. Soft warm tint, no exclamation marks, no badges or trophies.
- The iPhone chrome should feel **slightly hand-drawn** itself (matching the rest of the illustration style) — not a slick photoreal render. The Dynamic Island, the tab bar icons, the home indicator should all have the same gentle wobble as the cards.
- All prompt body text and the "Based on" lines are fictional. Designer is welcome to swap names or specifics if better-feeling alternatives come to mind, but **keep the shape of each prompt identical**:
  - Card 1: a moment of unstructured human connection during a trip + question about why unplanned > planned
  - Card 2: someone opened up about their work/ambitions + question about how you listen differently to ambition vs small talk
  - Card 3: a logistical decision under constraints + question about what your approach reveals about a personal trait
- Each "Based on" footer should reference 2–3 distinct evidence types: a specific recent event, a remembered pattern, and (where natural) a stated value or self-described trait of the user. That mix is what makes the prompts feel non-generic.

## Color hierarchy reference (consistent with the other two illustrations)
- **Blue chip** = today / situational
- **Lavender chip** = AI-detected pattern
- **Mint chip** = growth / value-touching
- **Warm-cream banner with orange accent** = streak / encouragement

- Background of the iPhone screen: very pale gray-cream so the white cards lift slightly off the surface
