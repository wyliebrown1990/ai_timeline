# Designer Brief: Hand-Drawn Illustration of the Amicai Daily Reflection Page

## Project context
Amicai is a personal AI relationship-and-life journal. The **Daily Reflection** page is the user's morning landing — one screen per day that surfaces the day's headline narrative, AI-noticed patterns, overdue relationship nudges with suggested messages, long-running life-event timelines, and journaling prompts. I need a hand-drawn illustration of this page, same template/format/styling as the attached screenshot, but with **fictional placeholder content** (the real text is private).

## Editorial direction (read before drawing)
The real page has ~13 sections — too many to render with equal weight in a single illustration. **Prioritize the cards below in full pictorial detail, and treat everything below the "Journal Prompts" card as quieter visual texture** (collapsed accordion strips or a "scroll continues" treatment). The story Amicai tells in the morning is the top of this page; the bottom is reference data.

**Tier 1 — render in full detail (this is the magic):**
1. Date navigator + headline quote (the day's one-sentence narrative)
2. Today's Focus card (intention check-in)
3. Something I Noticed card (AI pattern catch)
4. Relationship Movements card (the showpiece — overdue nudges with suggested messages)
5. Life Events card (long-running timeline check-in)
6. Day's Events card
7. Journal Prompts card

**Tier 2 — render as collapsed accordion rows, label-only:**
8. Conversation Highlights / Outreach Metrics / Score Breakdown
9. Upcoming Occasions
10. Soul File
11. Calls & FaceTime

The illustration should feel like the user just opened the page on a Thursday morning with coffee — the first 70% is rich and inviting, the last 30% trails off into useful-but-quiet reference.

## Visual style
- Hand-drawn / Excalidraw aesthetic: pencil or fine-marker linework, slightly imperfect strokes
- Muted color fills behind cards (soft cyan, lavender, mint, cream) — not flat brand colors
- Handwritten labels for section eyebrows; body text in a clean sketched sans
- Asymmetric, slightly wobbling rectangles. Avoid pixel-perfect alignment
- Background: off-white paper texture
- **Match the visual language of the companion `/journal` page illustration** so they read as a set

## Canvas
Single-column mobile-friendly layout, ~720px wide, ~2400px tall. (The page is content-tall, not split into columns — unlike the Journal page.)

## Tone & feel notes for the designer
- Top of the page should feel **like the morning** — gentle, contemplative, the kind of thing you read with a cup of coffee. Not productivity-app urgent.
- The **Relationship Movements card is the visual anchor** — give it the most generous spacing, the cleanest linework, the most visible "suggested message" boxes. This is the "Amicai noticed something for you" moment that defines the product.
- Color hierarchy:
  - Cyan tint = today's intention (Today's Focus)
  - Lavender tint = AI insight (Something I Noticed)
  - Orange/yellow accents = relationship signals (Needs Attention / Opportunity)
  - Mint accents = life-event progress (Life Events)
  - Pale violet accents = reflection prompts (Day's Events lightbulb, Journal Prompts)
- Tier 2 should fade slightly — lighter pencil weight, lower contrast — so the priority is unmistakable
- All names and body text are fictional. Designer is welcome to swap names if better-feeling alternatives come to mind, but **keep the shape of every piece of content identical** (same sentence rhythm, same number of clauses, same specificity of detail like "22 days past your biweekly contact goal" or "Six weeks later").
